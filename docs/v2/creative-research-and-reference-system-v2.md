## 2. Evaluation Matrix for Design Ideas

Before adopting any interaction or visual technique, it must be evaluated across five criteria:
1. **Brand Alignment**: Does it celebrate "To Inspire" and its golden star monogram, or is it arbitrary decoration?
2. **Usability Impact**: Does it enhance exploration or does it hijack user control?
3. **Performance Budget**: Does the WebGL scene load under 2 seconds on mobile 4G with <15% CPU load?
4. **Accessibility Score**: Can the experience be fully navigated with tab keys and screen readers?
5. **Longevity**: Will this look distinctive in 3 years, or is it an ephemeral monthly gimmick?
